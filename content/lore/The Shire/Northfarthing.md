The northernmost region of the Shire, the Northfarthing, is characterized by its cooler climate, snow-covered winters, and rugged landscapes. It is less populated than other regions, making it a quiet retreat for those who seek solitude or adventure.

### **Notable Locations**

- **Bindbole Wood**:  
    A vast evergreen forest that remains lush throughout the year. Its ancient trees are said to whisper secrets, and its dense heart is largely unexplored. Though no wolves have been seen here since the Fell Winter, tales of lurking shadows keep many Hobbits at bay.
- **Greenfields**:  
    A historic site where Bandobras "Bullroarer" Took famously defeated the Goblin horde from Mount Gram. This battle secured its place in Hobbit lore as a defining moment of bravery.
- **North Moors**:  
    Rolling, untamed lands where winter snows fall heaviest. Known for their stark beauty and as a haven for those seeking isolation.
