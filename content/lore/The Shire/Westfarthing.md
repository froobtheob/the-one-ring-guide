The Westfarthing is the heart of the Shire, home to its cultural and administrative centers. It is here that the traditions and values of the Hobbits are most visible.

### **Key Locations**

- **Michel Delving**:  
    The Shire’s largest town and its de facto capital. It houses the Mayor’s residence, the Town Hole, and the famous Mathom-house museum, which showcases Hobbit treasures and history.
- **Hobbiton**:  
    Perhaps the most iconic village in the Shire, Hobbiton is home to Bag End, the Party Tree, and the mill of Sandyman. Its rolling hills and friendly community embody the essence of Hobbit life.
- **Bywater**:  
    A social hub featuring the Green Dragon Inn, Bywater is where Hobbits gather to share stories, enjoy ale, and discuss local happenings.
- **Needlehole**:  
    Known for its bog-wood pipes, Needlehole is a northern village that combines Hobbit ingenuity with natural resources.

### **Other Locations**

- **Tuckborough**:  
    The ancestral home of the Took family and the seat of the Thain. The Great Smials, a vast Hobbit mansion, dominates this village.
- **Waymeet**:  
    A crossroads where Hobbits from across the Shire gather to trade, gossip, and share a pint at the Walking Party Tavern.
- **Little Delving**:  
    A quaint village near Michel Delving, known for its watchtower and tight-knit community.

### **Special Features**

- **Three-Farthing Stone**:  
    The geographic center of the Shire, marking the meeting point of the Westfarthing, Eastfarthing, and Southfarthing.
- **The Water**:  
    A river flowing through the Westfarthing, central to its agriculture and leisure.
