The agricultural heart of the Shire, the Southfarthing is renowned for its pipe-weed and wine, with its sprawling fields and gentle hills.

### **Notable Locations**

- **Longbottom**:  
    The birthplace of the Shire’s pipe-weed industry, this village produces the finest pipe-weed varieties, including Longbottom Leaf, Old Toby, and Southern Star. The farms here are a testament to Hobbit dedication and craftsmanship.
- **Pincup**:  
    Nestled in the Green Hill Country, Pincup is famed for its vineyards. Its wine, Old Winyards, is a strong red cherished across the Shire and beyond.
- **Sarn Ford**:  
    A stone crossing at the southernmost border of the Shire. Watched over by the Rangers of the North, it is a place of mystery and vigilance, with tales of hooded men and hidden dangers.

### **Special Features**

- **Shirebourn River**:  
    Flowing through the eastern border, the Shirebourn is a lifeline for angling enthusiasts and a serene feature of the Southfarthing.
- **Overbourn Marshes**:  
    A quiet, eerie expanse where mournful songs and strange noises drift over still waters. Few Hobbits dare to explore its depths.
