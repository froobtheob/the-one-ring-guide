A fertile and bustling region, the Eastfarthing connects the Shire to the outside world via the Brandywine Bridge. Its villages are vibrant, and its lands are known for their agricultural abundance.

### **Notable Locations**

- **Stock**:  
    A thriving village, home to the renowned Golden Perch inn, which serves some of the best ale in the Shire. Stock is a hub for travelers and locals alike.
- **Frogmorton**:  
    A peaceful village surrounded by marshy lands. Known for its inns, it’s a favorite stop for those traveling along the East Road.
- **The Yale**:  
    Fertile farmlands dotted with prosperous Hobbit communities. The Yale is known for its well-tended fields and friendly locals.
- **Budgeford**:  
    A picturesque village by the river, home to the influential Bolger family. The Water runs serenely through this area, making it a scenic and restful place.
