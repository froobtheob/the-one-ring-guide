An outlier to the Shire, Buckland lies across the Brandywine River. Its inhabitants, the Brandybucks, are known for their love of boats and unique customs.

### **Key Locations**

- **Brandy Hall**:  
    The sprawling home of the Brandybuck family, located in Bucklebury.
- **The High Hay**:  
    A tall hedge separating Buckland from the mysterious Old Forest, providing protection and peace of mind to its residents.
